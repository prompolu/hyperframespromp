# SARAPROC MAROC — storyboard

**Format:** 1920 × 1080, 16:9, 30 fps, 3 min 45 s. French on-screen text and no voiceover.

**Style:** Apple-keynote look in navy, blue, white and silver:
- silver headline type and white plates for the logos;
- glass cards with soft floor reflections and a slow camera drift;
- a light sheen that sweeps across each card.

**Music:** an original, energetic corporate track at 120 BPM, so one bar lasts 2 s. Every section starts on a downbeat.

**Between sections:**
- The music stops on beat 3 of the last bar, which leaves about 0.75 s of silence.
- The **LAB SARAPROC** card (PDF page 2) appears during that silence.
- The music comes back on the next downbeat.
- There are no sound effects.

**Sources:** All text, photos and certification names come from saraprocmaroc.com, except where noted ⚑.

## Points to check

- **⚑ Not found on the site:** *Fours*, *Trempage*, *Sanitaires* and *Écoles* come from your brief. I kept them, but you may want to confirm or remove them.
- **Pigments:** the brief says "pigments", but the site calls this category *Colorants*, so the film uses *Colorants*.
- **Medical:** the site also lists *Désinfection par voie aérienne* (aerosolisation). It is not in the film because your brief did not list it, but it can go in as a seventh card.
- **Second water section:** the site has only one water-treatment category. Section 11 uses the site's own water text (the problems it neutralises and where it is used) on a light theme, so it does not repeat section 6.
- **Official logo:** the file on the site is only 360 × 162 px and will show at about 1.4×. A vector or large PNG would give a sharper final frame.
