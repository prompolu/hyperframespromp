- **Pigments:** the brief says "pigments", but the site calls this category *Colorants*, so the film uses *Colorants*.
- **Medical:** the site also lists *Désinfection par voie aérienne*. It is not in the film because your brief did not list it.
- **Second water section:** it uses the site's own water text (the problems it neutralises and the installations it serves) on a light theme, so it does not repeat section 6.
- **Official logo:** the file on the site is only 360 × 162 px and shows at 1.4×. A vector or large PNG would give a sharper final frame.
